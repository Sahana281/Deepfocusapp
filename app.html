Welcome to DeepFocus!

Here's a quick guide to get you started

1
Start the Timer

Click the ▶ Start button to begin your focus session. The timer is set to 2 minutes for this demo.

2
Track Distractions

Got distracted? Click 😵 I got distracted to log it. This adds 30 seconds as a gentle penalty.

3
Take Breaks

When the timer ends, a ☕ break automatically starts. Breaks help you stay productive longer!

4
Track Your Progress

Check the Insights panel on the left to see your focus time, streak, and distractions.

5
Customize Settings

Click ⚙️ Settings to adjust focus duration, break times, and more.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Space to Start/Pause the timer
  • S to Skip current block
  • ? to see all keyboard shortcuts
