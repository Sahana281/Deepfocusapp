📖 User Manual

Welcome to DeepFocus! This guide will help you learn how to use the app.

➕

Adding Tasks

Click the "Add New Task" button on the left side. Give your task a name (like "Math Homework") and set how many minutes you want to focus. You can also add a deadline if needed!

🎯

Starting a Focus Session

After adding a task, click the "🎯 Start" button next to it. Then click the big "▶ Start" button in the center timer to begin focusing!

⏸️

Timer Controls

  • ▶ Start: Begin your focus session
  • ⏸ Pause: Pause the timer if you need a break
  • ⏭ Skip: Skip to the next task if you want to move on
⚠️

Tracking Distractions

If you get distracted, click the "⚠️ I got distracted" button. Choose what distracted you (like Phone, Boredom, or Hunger). This helps you learn what pulls you away from focusing!

⭐

Reflecting After Sessions

When you finish a focus session, you'll be asked to rate how well you focused (1-5 stars) and write a note. You can also choose what helped you focus, like "Quiet Space" or "Music".

📊

Insights & Patterns

Check the right side panel to see your progress! The Patterns tab shows your top distractions and what helps you focus. The Feedback tab shows your productivity trends.

🎯

Daily Goals

Set a daily focus goal at the bottom of the right panel. This helps you stay motivated! You'll see a progress bar showing how close you are to your goal.

🏆

Badges & Achievements

Earn badges as you use the app! Click the 🏆 button in the top bar to see your badges. Unlock achievements like "First Focus", "Streak Master", and more!

⚙️

Settings

Click the ⚙️ button to adjust sound settings and other preferences.

💡 Quick Tips

  • Press Space to start or pause the timer
  • Press S to skip the current task
  • Try to focus for at least 15 minutes at a time
  • Take notes after each session to remember what worked
